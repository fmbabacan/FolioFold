# Code of Conduct

FolioFold contributors and participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus feedback on the work, welcome constructive disagreement, and avoid harassment, threats, personal attacks, or disclosure of private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations. Conduct concerns can be reported privately to [fatihmehmet@babacan.co](mailto:fatihmehmet@babacan.co).
