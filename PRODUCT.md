# FolioFold

## Register

product

## Users

FolioFold serves freelancers, independent professionals, and small businesses working with PDFs throughout the day. They need to create repeatable documents, open and revise existing files, sign forms, and combine or extract pages without uploading sensitive material or learning a complex publishing suite.

## Product Purpose

FolioFold is a lightweight, open-source macOS workspace for creating and working with PDFs entirely on-device. Success means common document tasks feel immediate, understandable, and safe while the application stays small, responsive, and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The interface should feel focused and quietly capable, with the familiarity and information density of Notion adapted to native macOS conventions.

## Anti-references

- Dense Acrobat-style toolbars that expose every command simultaneously.
- Decorative glass effects, oversized rounded cards, heavy shadows, gradients, and dashboard-like metric layouts.
- Custom controls that replace familiar macOS behavior without a clear workflow benefit.
- Bright inactive states, ornamental motion, and visual noise that competes with the document.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the document central and reveal tools in context.
2. Use familiar macOS interactions so the interface disappears into the task.
3. Protect source files and make destructive actions explicit.
4. Prefer native frameworks and measured simplicity over speculative abstractions.
5. Earn trust through offline operation, clear limitations, and verifiable outcomes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, VoiceOver labels, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, system text settings, and non-color status cues are release requirements. Layout and localization infrastructure must accommodate longer translations and right-to-left languages even though v1 ships in English.
